How to calibrate
  1. Drag the two teal dots so the line lies exactly across the doorway people walk through.
  2. Check the IN / OUT labels point to the correct sides (inside vs outside the office).
  3. If they're backwards, press Flip IN / OUT.
  4. Press Save Line. It takes effect instantly — no restart.
The camera is mounted outside the entrance: an employee waves from outside to check in on arrival, and again (still outside, having just stepped out) to check out when leaving. The line marks the doorway, and the IN side must point into the office so the system knows which side is outside.
Camera Image
On by default — a mirrored view is what people expect when waving at a screen. If you turn this on/off after calibrating, the IN/OUT sides may swap; just press Flip IN / OUT above if that happens.

To use your phone as the camera: install DroidCam (or IP Webcam) on the phone, connect the phone and this PC to the same Wi-Fi, open the app, and enter the IP it shows. Just the IP (e.g. 192.168.1.7) is enough — it expands to DroidCam's default stream automatically. Takes effect immediately, no restart.
Minimum Shift Length
After checking in, an employee can't check out until this many hours have passed (default 1). This also stops an accidental second wave right after arriving from checking someone straight back out. Set to 0 to allow check-out any time.
Attendance Day Boundary
An employee can check in and check out once per work day. This is the hour a new work day starts — not midnight. Example: with a boundary of 6 (6am), someone who checks out at 8pm cannot check back in at 12:05am; the cycle only resets once 6am arrives.
